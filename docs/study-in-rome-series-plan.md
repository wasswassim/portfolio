# Plan: "Study in Rome from Tunisia" blog series (Lazio)

Written 2026-10-09. Status: **plan only, nothing built.** Wait for the user's approval and answers to section 9.
Research: 5 agents + manual checks on official sources only (pages read 2026-10-09). The user's Gemini report was used
as a starting list, not as a source: several of its claims were wrong (see section 8).

## 0. Key facts that shape the series

- **Target cycle = 2027/2028.** Today all 2026/27 deadlines are gone (DiSCo closed 22 Jul 2026, Sapienza pre-selection
  closed 15 May 2026). The MUR circular already covers 2027/28 (visa deadline 31 Oct 2027). Other 2027/28 dates are
  projected from the 2026/27 calendar and must be written as "expected, check the new call" until published.
- **Universities covered:** Sapienza, Tor Vergata, Roma Tre (public, Rome). Bachelor (Laurea), Master (Laurea Magistrale),
  PhD (Dottorato). Medicine is excluded (new 2025 access reform, rules for internationals not published).
- **Tunisia is a Hague Apostille country** (in force 30 Mar 2018; apostille by Tunisian notaries since 1 Mar 2019, 10-20 TND),
  **not** a Lisbon Recognition Convention party (CoE chart ETS 165, checked 2026-10-09) -> CIMEA needs Comparability **+**
  Verification (EUR 300) unless the university accepts the Embassy's Dichiarazione di Valore.
- **New Embassy rule from 30 Mar 2026:** education titles must first be authenticated by the competent Tunisian ministry,
  then certified copies at the municipality, then apostille, then Italian translation.

## 4. Part-by-part content outline (with the links to include)

### Part 2: Choose a university and apply
1. How admission works for non-EU students resident abroad (university first, then Universitaly).
2. Find a course: https://www.universitaly.it/cerca-corsi ; each university's international page:
   Sapienza https://www.uniroma1.it/en/en/admissions , Tor Vergata https://web.uniroma2.it/en/percorso/admissions ,
   Roma Tre https://orientamento.uniroma3.it/en/about-roma-tre/international-students-admissions/
3. Language: Italian-taught = Italian test B2 by the university (exempt with a B2 certificate); English-taught = B2 certificate.
4. Apply on each portal (baby steps): Sapienza MoveIn https://sapienza.gomovein.com/ ; Tor Vergata Delphi
   https://delphi.uniroma2.it/ ; Roma Tre GOMP https://portalestudente.uniroma3.it/ . Fees, tests (SAT/TOLC where required).
5. After admission: Universitaly pre-enrolment, steps A/B/C (name exactly as passport, Embassy in Tunis, purpose "study"),
   login https://universitaly-private.cineca.it/index.php/login , FAQ PDF. New 2026/27 cap on validations per university.
6. PhD: one call per university, typically spring (Sapienza closed 17 Jun 2026, Tor Vergata 9 Jun 2026, Roma Tre 14 Jul 2026),
   calls pages for each; Universitaly pre-enrolment also required.
7. FAQ + sources (Universitaly, MUR circular PDF, the three universities).

### Part 3: Prepare, legalise and translate documents
1. The 30 Mar 2026 Embassy rule, quoted.
2. Step by step per document: bac -> Ministry of Education; university degrees -> Ministry of Higher Education (rectorates);
   BTP/BTS -> Ministry of Employment and Vocational Training; health titles -> Ministry of Health; private institutions ->
   equivalence decision. Then certified copies (front/back) at the municipality, apostille by a notary, Italian translation.
   Full school path required, name exactly as in the passport.
3. Translation: Embassy's list of known translators (PDF on ambtunisi.esteri.it); whether the translation is also apostilled (Q4).
4. Academic value: DOV (form PDF, booking on https://prenotami.esteri.it/ , EUR 50 or free for study, legalizza.tunisi@esteri.it)
   vs CIMEA (https://cimea-diplome.it/ , EUR 300, up to 60 working days; Tor Vergata has its own CIMEA portal).
   Which university accepts which (Sapienza: CIMEA C+V, DOV accepted; Tor Vergata: either; Roma Tre: either).
5. Civil-status documents for visa and DiSCo: birth extract (municipality / e-houwiya, idaraty.tn), family record, residence.
6. Timeline + checklist table; warning about paid "fast appointment" offers (Embassy notice 11 Jul 2025).

### Part 4: DiSCo Lazio scholarship
1. What it gives a fuori sede student: EUR 6,571.11 (up to EUR 7,556.78; min EUR 3,285.60) + 2 free meals/day, housing place
   option (~EUR 200-298/month deducted), tuition rules depend on the university (Tor Vergata flat fees still apply).
2. Eligibility: enrolled by ~10 Feb; ISEEUP <= EUR 28,339.88 and ISPEUP <= EUR 61,608.48 (2026/27 values); first-years ranked
   on economic data only; separate non-EU ranking; no same-level degree already held.
3. Register without SPID: https://dirstudio.laziodisco.it/registrazione/index , then https://login.laziodisco.it/access/borse
4. **Family economic documents** (the hard part): family composition; gross income of each member (year N-2), no-income stated
   explicitly; real estate with surface area; financial assets at 31/12; rent paid. Total family income cannot be zero.
   No self-certification. Issuer + legalisation: see Q1. Translation into Italian mandatory.
5. ISEE parificato: only at a DiSCo partner CAF (list PDF), free, identity check done there, deadline ~10 Dec.
6. Calendar (projected 2027/28) and how to keep the grant (20 credits by ~10 Aug; IBAN within 6 months; PEC/domicile).
7. Sources: bando PDF + English version, Allegato L (deadlines), Allegato E (Tunisia not on the "poor countries" list),
   international FAQ, translation/legalisation page, CAF list. Contact 06.49701, urp@laziodisco.it.

### Part 5: Student visa and blocked account
1. How it starts: Universitaly validation -> Almaviva emails the date (no self-booking; date cannot change). AVS address,
   hours, phones; Embassy does not take applications by email.
2. The Embassy's 15-item checklist (July 2026 sheet, EN/FR version linked).
3. Blocked amount: EUR 10,179.85; the bank file under BCT circular 2025-10 (CIN, bac, boursier/non-boursier attestation,
   (pre-)registration certificate, repatriation commitment; one bank per year; advance transfer for a blocked account Art. 14;
   refund rules Art. 26 if the visa is refused). Original "dichiarazione di blocage" signed and stamped + RIB.
4. Insurance (no fixed euro minimum; 3 accepted forms), accommodation proof (residence application is not valid proof).
5. Fees and timing: EUR 50 + 68.300 TND; up to 90 days; deadline 31 Oct 2027; visa valid 365 days (100 if "con riserva").
6. Sources: Embassy notice (1 Jun 2026), info sheet PDFs, fees page, where-to-apply page, MUR circular, BCT circular.

### Part 6: Arriving in Rome
1. Within 8 working days: residence permit kit (yellow band) at a post office with Sportello Amico; contents, costs, receipt,
   Questura appointment for fingerprints, status check https://questure.poliziadistato.it/stranieri/ ; Sapienza police desk.
2. Codice fiscale: assigned by the Questura with the permit; earlier if needed at any Agenzia delle Entrate office with form AA4/8.
3. Bank account: right to a "conto di base" (Banca d'Italia page); Postepay Evolution; a bank needs the codice fiscale.
4. Finalise enrolment at your university (originals check, permit receipt, fee payment) - one paragraph per university.
5. Health: SSN voluntary registration or insurance.
6. DiSCo after arrival: permit upload, CAF appointment and ISEEUP signature, IBAN, PEC.
7. Checklist "first 30 days".

### Part 7: Finding accommodation in Rome
DiSCo residences (only through the yearly call) and partner residences; university housing services (Sapienza Sturent desk,
Tor Vergata housing@uniroma2.it, Roma Tre accommodation service); platforms: HousingAnywhere, Uniplaces, Spotahome,
Immobiliare.it, Idealista, Subito.it (universities list student discount codes); lease registration within 30 days
(Agenzia delle Entrate, RLI); scam checklist (Polizia Postale advice). Photos gallery of Rome neighbourhoods near campuses.

## 8. Corrections to the Gemini report (do not reuse these claims)

- The student does **not** book the Almaviva appointment for the university-study visa; Almaviva emails a date after validation.
- The EUR 30,000 insurance minimum is a Schengen rule; the Embassy asks for unlimited urgent hospital cover in one of 3 forms.
- "Sworn translators from the Embassy list are mandatory": not stated; the Embassy calls it a list of "known" translators.
- "Free CIMEA comparability via ARDI for Lisbon countries": Tunisia is not a Lisbon party.
- Visa fee EUR 116 is the standard D fee; the study D visa is EUR 50.

## 9. User decisions (2026-10-09)

1. **DiSCo family documents:** Tunisian documents are enough (no Italian-Embassy certificate), with a legitimate translation
   by a legitimate translator. (User's experience; the bando wording "autorità diplomatiche italiane" stays a footnote:
   "if in doubt, confirm with DiSCo".)
2. **Apostille on both** the original and the translation. The translator must be on the Italian Embassy's list or the
   Tunisian government list of sworn translators.
3. **Blocked account:** the full amount is blocked locally in Tunisia. After enrolment in Italy the student sends the
   enrolment papers to Tunisia; the bank then lets the parents transfer, up to **4,000 TND per month** (matches BCT
   circular 2025-10 living allowance).
4. **Add private universities LUISS and LUMSA** (research pending).
5. **Tables:** yes, simple ones.
6. **Languages:** en / fr / ar.
7. **New content requested:**
   - A small table on spending the **6,000 TND installation allowance** (BCT circular 2025-10: up to 6,000 TND per academic
     year) carefully during the first month, until the other steps are done and the student may find student work
     (e.g. restaurants), using apps such as Subito.
   - A notice on scholarship payments: if the ISEEUP is completed correctly after arrival, the first instalment arrives
     around December/January and the second around May; funding can be late, but not usually by more than a month.
     **Conflict to resolve:** the 2026/27 bando says first-year Bachelor: 20% by 10 Nov + 30% by 27 Dec, balance (50%)
     from October of the next year after 20 credits by 10 Aug; first-year Master/PhD: 50% by 27 Dec, balance from October;
     later years: 50% by 27 Dec, balance from June.

## 9b. Extra research (2026-10-09)

- **LUISS (private):** non-EU route = Luiss Test or international certifications; **2027/28: applications Oct 2026 -
  10 Feb 2027, test 22-26 Feb 2027, enrol by 4 May 2027, fee EUR 150**; flat Single Fee **EUR 15,000/yr** (bachelor,
  2027/28) + regional tax; non-EU scholarships exist (20 full waivers in 2025/26; 2027/28 call not out). Universitaly still
  required for the visa. URLs in the agent report (luiss.it admission + fees pages).
- **LUMSA (private):** residents abroad pay a flat **EUR 4,390/yr** (2026/27 guide); bachelor courses in Italian (B2);
  online admission test EUR 100; Master's apply page lumsa.it/en/apply-to-enroll (EUR 100); admission letter then Universitaly.
- **Student work:** max **20 h/week, 1,040 h/year** (art. 14 c.4 DPR 394/1999, Normattiva). Working while holding only the
  first-permit postal receipt: art. 5 c.9-bis TU (as amended by DL 146/2025) allows stay and work pending the first issue
  once the receipt exists; not stated specifically for students -> write "check with the Questura/university before starting".
  Job channels: Sapienza JobTeaser, Tor Vergata placement.uniroma2.it, Roma Tre Jobsoul, Regione Lazio job offers and
  SpazioLavoro, Subito (offerte lavoro), InfoJobs, Indeed, LinkedIn. Link the Ministry of Labour anti-exploitation page.
- **Sworn translators:** Tunisian Ministry of Justice lists by district / by language (justice.gov.tn ?id=369 / ?id=368);
  Embassy list PDF 25-10-2024.
- **Rate:** BCT 7 Oct 2026, 1 EUR = 3.3671 TND -> 6,000 TND ~ EUR 1,782; 4,000 TND ~ EUR 1,188 (write "about", with date).
