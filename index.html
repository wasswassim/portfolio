Who am I?

I'm Wassim Gatri, a Tunisian full stack entrepreneur living between Tunis and Rome. I'm currently documenting my experience and the solutions I found in my blog, Building a Life in Italy: free guides, checked against official sources, for Tunisians who study, work and settle in Italy. My background is in Business Information Systems, and I'm studying for a Master's in Digital Marketing at LUMSA University in Rome. I design and build websites and apps, plan and analyse marketing campaigns, and work with agentic engineering skills: AI agents like Claude Code help me ship faster, while every decision stays mine. I speak 4 languages: Arabic, French, English and Italian.
