How much does studying in Rome cost for a Tunisian student? The 2026/27 first-year budget

Every official fee from Tunis to your first year at Sapienza, in euros and dinars, with rent and the money you can transfer

By Wassim GatriPublished October 15, 2026Updated October 15, 20266 min readLast verified October 15, 2026Guide ↓

In short · On this page

A Tunisian student's first year at Sapienza in Rome costs about EUR 1,626 (about 5,476 TND) in fixed official fees in 2026/27: visa, application, residence permit, university fees, health service, transport and a bank card. With a private single room at EUR 500 to 700 a month, the year comes to EUR 7,626 to 10,026 before food, while the EUR 10,179.85 blocked for the visa stays your own money to live on.

How much money do you need for your first year as a Tunisian student in Rome?

Short answer: about EUR 1,626.46 (about 5,476 TND) in fixed official costs for the first year at Sapienza in 2026/27, then rent on top. With a private single room, the year comes to EUR 7,626.46 – EUR 10,026.46 before food and personal spending.

This page adds up every fee our guides quote, from the visa in Tunis to the first year of university, using one example: a Bachelor's or Master's student at Sapienza in the lowest fee band, the flat fee for students from Tunisia. The blocked amount for the visa (EUR 10,179.85) is not in the total: it is your own money, released to you every month to live on.

1. How much does the first year in Rome cost for a Tunisian student?

The first year in Rome costs a Tunisian student about EUR 1,626.46 in fixed official fees, plus EUR 6,000 – EUR 8,400 for 12 months in a private room. Each line below is detailed in the next sections.

2. What do you pay in Tunisia before you leave?

Before you leave Tunisia, you pay the university application and the study visa: EUR 80, plus the 68.300 TND Almaviva service fee. The visa is paid in cash in dinars at the visa centre in Tunis, so the dinar amount follows the exchange rate.

Your documents add costs that have no fixed price: a Tunisian notary charges 10 or 20 TND per apostille, and sworn translators set their own rates. The Dichiarazione di Valore is free for study, while a CIMEA statement costs EUR 300 when your university asks for it. See who can translate your Tunisian documents into Italian and whether you need a DOV or a CIMEA statement.

3. What do you pay in your first weeks in Rome?

In your first weeks in Rome, the residence permit kit and a first Italian card cost EUR 121.46. You apply for the permesso di soggiorno at a post office within 8 working days of arrival: the revenue stamp is bought at a tobacconist, and the rest is paid at the post office.

4. How much are university fees in the first year?

At Sapienza, the first year costs EUR 456 for a student from Tunisia in the lowest band: the flat fee, the Lazio regional tax and a revenue stamp. Private universities cost far more: LUMSA's yearly tuition is EUR 4,390, regional tax included.

5. How much does a student pay for rent in Rome?

Rent is the largest cost: a standard private single room near a Rome university costs EUR 500 – EUR 700 a month in 2026, against EUR 200 – EUR 298 for a DiSCo residence place, which only DiSCo scholarship winners get.

6. Which yearly costs come on top: health, transport and bank?

Health, transport and a bank card add EUR 969 a year: EUR 700 for voluntary registration with the national health service, the ATAC annual pass and the Postepay yearly fee.

The EUR 700 health line is the upper case: private health insurance valid in Italy for the whole permit is accepted instead, and in my experience a policy taken in Tunisia through the bank that holds your study file replaced the SSN registration. SSN registration covers one calendar year, to 31 December: if you arrive in the autumn and stay with the SSN, a second payment is due for the new year. See the arrival guide.

7. Is the blocked amount for the visa a cost?

No: the EUR 10,179.85 blocked for the 2026/27 study visa is your own money. A Tunisian bank blocks it for a year and releases it at EUR 783.06 a month for 13 months, which is the Italian Embassy's own estimate of what a student needs to live on.

Food and personal spending are not in this budget because there is no official figure for them. The monthly release of the blocked amount is the benchmark to plan with. See how the blocked account works with a Tunisian bank.

8. How much money can you send from Tunisia?

Under Central Bank of Tunisia circular 2025-10, a student's study file allows a one-off installation allowance of 6,000 TND and up to 4,000 TND a month for living costs, through one Tunisian bank per academic year.

9. How much of this can the DiSCo scholarship cover?

A DiSCo Lazio scholarship pays an out-of-town student EUR 6,571.11 a year in 2026/27, and up to EUR 7,556.78 for the lowest incomes, plus meals. That covers a large part of the first-year budget. A DiSCo residence place is then deducted from it.

Frequently asked questions

How much money do I need to study in Italy as a Tunisian student?

For the first year at Sapienza in Rome in 2026/27: about EUR 1,626.46 (about 5,476 TND) in fixed official fees, plus EUR 500 – EUR 700 a month for a private room, food and personal spending. You also block EUR 10,179.85 for the visa, which is your own money to live on.

Is studying in Italy expensive for Tunisians?

Public universities are cheap for students from Tunisia: Sapienza's first year costs EUR 456 in the lowest band. Rent is the main cost in Rome, and the DiSCo scholarship (EUR 6,571.11 a year in 2026/27) can cover most of it if you qualify.

How much is the study visa for Italy in dinars?

The study visa costs EUR 50, paid in cash in dinars at the Almaviva centre in Tunis, plus the 68.300 TND service fee. The dinar amount follows the exchange rate, so check the Embassy's fees page before your appointment.

Do I have to spend the blocked amount?

No. The EUR 10,179.85 stays yours: the bank releases EUR 783.06 a month for 13 months, and that monthly money is what you live on in Italy.

How much is a student room in Rome?

About EUR 500 – EUR 700 a month for a standard private single room near a university in 2026, less near Tor Vergata and more in the most sought-after areas. A DiSCo residence costs EUR 200 – EUR 298 a month for scholarship winners.

  1. Prepare your documents (apostille, translation) in Tunisia
