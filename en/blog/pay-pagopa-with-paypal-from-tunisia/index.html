How to Pay a pagoPA Notice from Tunisia

With PayPal or an international card

You can pay an Italian pagoPA notice from Tunisia on checkout.pagopa.it, without SPID, by typing the Codice Avviso and the creditor's tax code and choosing PayPal or a Visa, Mastercard or other international card. Important notice

This guide is for information only and is not legal or financial advice. Payment methods, provider fees and Tunisian exchange rules can change, so always check the official sources linked below before you pay.

Your Italian university sent you a pagoPA notice, but you are still in Tunisia. Can you pay it with PayPal or a Tunisian international card?

Usually, yes. Open checkout.pagopa.it↗ (opens in a new tab), type the Codice Avviso and the Codice Fiscale Ente Creditore (or scan the QR code), and choose PayPal or a credit, debit or prepaid card. You do not need SPID, an Italian bank account or a codice fiscale of your own to pay a notice.

One honest caveat: PayPal is an official pagoPA method, but no official source confirms that a Tunisia-registered PayPal account goes through. Test with a small notice first and keep a card ready as a backup.

This is part 3 of our guide to paying Italian fees from Tunisia, which compares every way to pay. For the full list of study costs, see what studying in Italy costs.

1. What is pagoPA, and which student fees use it?

pagoPA is the Italian public payment system. A public body (a university, the Lazio region, a ministry) issues a payment notice, the avviso di pagamento, and you pay it through a payment provider (PSP) of your choice. The provider, not pagoPA, sets and collects the fee.

  • Sapienza: you generate the payment on Infostud and pay it only through pagoPA; the rules say no other method is accepted (Sapienza fee rules 2026/27↗ (opens in a new tab)).
  • Tor Vergata: the EUR 30 application fee is paid through pagoPA (application procedure↗ (opens in a new tab)), and enrolment fees are generated on Delphi.
  • Lazio regional tax: EUR 140 plus a EUR 16 stamp, paid with the first instalment by almost every student at a public university in Lazio, DiSCo applicants included; exemptions are listed in your university's fee rules (Sapienza: regional tax and stamp↗ (opens in a new tab)).

2. What you need before you pay

  • The notice: the PDF from Infostud, Delphi or another portal, with the 18-digit Codice Avviso and the Codice Fiscale Ente Creditore (the creditor's tax code), or its QR code. If you do not have it, ask the university: pagoPA cannot generate it for you.
  • An email address: the outcome of the payment is sent there.
  • A way to pay: a PayPal account with a confirmed linked card, or an international Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Visa Electron or American Express card enabled for online and international payments.
  • Enough allowance on the card: the international technology card (CTI) is capped at 1,000 TND a year per person.

To use PayPal, link your international card to the account first; our guide to the international technology card in Tunisia explains how to get one. PayPal may place a small temporary charge to confirm the card and refunds it after verification (PayPal Tunisia fees↗ (opens in a new tab)).

A Tunisian PayPal account is a contract with PayPal Pte. Ltd., a Singapore company. It lets you pay and send; receiving money is offered only “where available”, and PayPal's Tunisian site promotes only paying and sending (PayPal user agreement, Tunisia↗ (opens in a new tab)). That is not a problem here, because your goal is to pay.

Watch the 1,000 TND cap

The example notice above (about 525 TND) uses about half of a year's CTI allowance, and the CTI's list of allowed uses does not name university fees. Read what the technology card may pay for before you rely on it for tuition.

3. How to pay a pagoPA notice with PayPal, step by step

These steps follow the official pagoPA article Pagare con PayPal↗ (opens in a new tab).

Step 1: open checkout.pagopa.it

Go to checkout.pagopa.it↗ (opens in a new tab), or click the pagoPA button on your university portal. There is no login and no SPID.

Step 2: enter the notice codes

Scan the QR code, or type the Codice Avviso and the Codice Fiscale Ente Creditore, then press Vai al pagamento. Check that the amount and the creditor's name match your notice.

Step 3: type your email twice

Use an address you check: the outcome and the receipt details arrive there.

Step 4: choose PayPal and a provider

Select PayPal (anche a rate). If several providers are offered (Intesa Sanpaolo, Mooney or Nexi), pick one, check the total with the fee included, and press Paga.

Step 5: log in to PayPal and pay in full

You are sent to PayPal. Log in, choose your card or balance, and choose to pay in full: PayPal's instalments on pagoPA require you to live in Italy (PayPal Italy: pagoPA↗ (opens in a new tab)).

Step 6: read the outcome

Back on pagoPA, the result is shown on screen and sent by email. Then save the receipt (section 6).

What paying with PayPal adds

On top of the notice amount. The pagoPA fee is set by the provider and can change.

The fee of about 4% applies when PayPal converts the currency for you. Whether PayPal or your Tunisian bank converts a dinar card could not be confirmed, so compare the final amount on your bank statement after your test payment.

Test with a small notice first

A Tunisian PayPal account on pagoPA is not officially confirmed, and pagoPA says methods can vary by notice and provider. Try a small amount first (for example a EUR 16 stamp, if your university issues it as a separate notice) well before the deadline, and keep a card as a backup.

4. Paying with an international card instead

pagoPA accepts credit, debit and prepaid cards on the American Express, Maestro, Mastercard, Visa and Visa Electron circuits, whatever the issuing country (payment methods↗ (opens in a new tab)).

  1. After your email, choose Carta di credito o debito.
  2. Type the holder's name, card number, expiry date and CVV.
  3. Choose a provider; it does not have to be your bank.
  4. Check the amount with the fee included, then press Paga.
  5. Approve the payment on your bank's page or app (the 3-D Secure step).

Compare providers on the official cost transparency page↗ (opens in a new tab). If your card has no CVV, pagoPA says it is probably not enabled for online payments (paying by card↗ (opens in a new tab)).

5. Card declined or payment failed: what to do

pagoPA's own help page for foreign cards↗ (opens in a new tab) gives three fixes, in this order:

  1. Check the card's restrictions: international and online payments must be enabled. Look in your banking app or call your bank.
  2. Change the provider: a provider can refuse a card because of its own policy. On the summary screen, press modifica next to the suggested provider and pick another.
  3. Change the method: if the card fails, try PayPal, and if PayPal fails, try the card directly.

Also check that your CTI still has allowance left this year, and that you can receive your bank's confirmation code while you pay.

If money left your account but the outcome is negative, the provider may be holding it and will refund it, possibly after a few days (debit without payment↗ (opens in a new tab)). Check the due date and pay again through another method so you do not miss it. If nothing works, see the bank transfer option in our payment guide.

6. Keep the receipt and check the validation

Download the receipt (ricevuta) as soon as the payment succeeds and keep the PDF with the email. Some offices ask for it as proof of payment.

The university then validates the payment on its portal, often after a few days. Check Infostud or Delphi; if nothing appears after about a week, send the receipt to the student office. Tor Vergata warns that if the regional tax payment is not validated, you are not enrolled and you lose your DiSCo benefits (Tor Vergata student guide 2026/27↗ (opens in a new tab)).

Frequently asked questions

Does pagoPA accept foreign cards?

Yes. Credit, debit and prepaid Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Visa Electron and American Express cards are accepted, as long as the card is enabled for online and international payments and passes the 3-D Secure check.

Can I pay pagoPA in instalments from Tunisia?

No, not with PayPal: its instalments on pagoPA require you to live in Italy. Choose to pay in full when PayPal asks.

Do I need SPID to pay a pagoPA notice?

No. On checkout.pagopa.it you only need the Codice Avviso and the Codice Fiscale Ente Creditore (or the QR code), an email address and a payment method.

How much does it cost to pay pagoPA with PayPal?

EUR 1.50 up to EUR 248.50 and EUR 2.50 above (as of October 2026), plus about 4% if PayPal converts the currency for a Tunisian account.

How long until the university sees my payment?

Often a few days. Keep the receipt, check Infostud or Delphi, and contact the student office if the payment still does not appear after about a week.

I lost my Codice Avviso. Can pagoPA send it to me?

No. Only the body that issued the notice can give it to you, so download it again from your university portal or ask the student office.
